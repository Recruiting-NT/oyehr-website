About our HR
management system

We are building HR software that is clear to use, so people teams can focus on people instead of spreadsheets. OyeHR is built by Nextenti to bring employee records, leave, attendance and payroll into one simple system. We design every screen around the people who use it, so HR teams get accurate records and employees get answers without waiting.

What OyeHR does

OyeHR brings employee management, attendance and shifts, leave management, payroll, and reports and insights into one system. HR teams keep profiles, documents and job details organised in a single searchable directory, record working hours, assign shifts and see who is in, out or on leave. Employees request time off and managers approve it, with everything shown on a shared team calendar. Payslips are prepared for each pay run and reviewed before they go out, and a dashboard tracks headcount, attendance and approvals in one place.

OUR MISSIONMake everyday HR work simple, accurate and transparent for both the HR team and every employee.
OUR VISIONA workplace where managing people feels as easy as the work itself.

Frequently asked questions

Who is OyeHR for?

HR and people teams who want employee records, leave, attendance and payroll in one place.

How do employees request leave?

Employees submit a leave request from their account, and their manager approves it. Approved leave appears on the shared team calendar.

Can we review payroll before it is sent?

Yes. Payslips are prepared for each pay run and reviewed by your team before they go out.

Is employee information kept private?

Yes. Employee information is sensitive and access stays limited to the people who need it.
